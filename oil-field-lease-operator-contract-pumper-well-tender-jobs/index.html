Our Oil Field Lease Operator Jobs Featured in E&P Magazine...

Part of getting you more wells means building a tool in which oil companies can find you. We knocked that out with the Pumper Mesh.

But if no one knows the directory exists, what good will it do you?

So we asked ourselves: "How could we get our contract pumpers in front of as many oil companies as possible?"

Right then, the postman entered our office carrying a sign sent straight from the heavens. On top of that newly delivered stack of mail sat one of the most authoritative sources in oil and gas: a copy of E&P Magazine, with more than 35,000 decision makers at oil and gas companies flipping through its pages each month.

We tracked down Rhonda Duey (the editor of E&P) and pitched her, not an article about the GreaseBook app, but a story on how GreaseBook aims to introduce companies to the contract pumpers who are equipped with it. She liked the idea so much she featured the Pumper Mesh in an article aptly entitled "Operator: Unchained".

Please accept that article as a token of our appreciation for allowing GreaseBook to sit shotgun on your daily route. Here's to getting YOU more wells!
